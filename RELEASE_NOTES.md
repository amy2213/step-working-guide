# Release Notes - v1.0

**Approved:** October 10, 2026

## Complete master workbook

- 243 pages
- 12 steps
- 460 live reflection fields
- all fields multiline
- no `/MaxLen` character restrictions
- native selectable and searchable text
- clickable master contents
- step and section bookmarks
- each major section begins on a fresh page

## Source-fidelity audit

The final release was checked against the original 96-page source guide. The audit identified narrative that had been condensed or omitted in portions of the earlier structured browser dataset.

Corrections were incorporated before release, including audited/rebuilt content in Steps 5, 6, 8, and 9. Steps 10, 11, and 12 were reconstructed source-first from the original pages before their fillable PDFs were generated.

The approved PDF is therefore the canonical v1.0 release.

## Form QA

The combined master was checked for:

- unique field names
- multiline form flags
- zero character limits
- widget geometry and page bounds
- save / close / reopen persistence
- long-answer persistence
- PDFium rendering
- Poppler rendering
- native-text search/select behavior
